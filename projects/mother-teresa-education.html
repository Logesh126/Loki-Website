01 / Project overview

Designing a digital home for future educators.

The college’s stated purpose connects quality teacher education with rural upliftment. The website should make that purpose understandable while helping visitors find academic and institutional information.

Mother Terasa College of Education is located in Illuppur, Tamil Nadu. The institutional content supplied for this case study states that the college was established in 2005 and offers Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) and Master of Education (M.Ed) programmes.

The redesign brings together the institution’s background, vision and mission, academic pathways, recognition information, teaching practice, facilities and admissions in a clearer responsive experience.

“Rural Upliftment through Quality Education for Today and Tomorrow.”Institutional motto supplied in the college material
02 / The challenge

Making teacher education easier to understand and explore.

A teacher education website needs to explain more than course names. Visitors need a coherent view of academic pathways, eligibility, practical teaching experience, institutional credibility and the learning environment.

04 / Research & discovery

Understanding the audience.

The experience was framed around three likely visitor groups and the information each needs to make progress.

The personas below are proposed design personas based on likely website needs. They are not documented interview participants or validated research findings.

Persona 01Aspiring teacher

“I want to understand which programme is right for me.”

Goals
Explore B.Ed and M.Ed · Understand eligibility · Learn about teaching practice · Discover career pathways.

UX needs
Clear course details, accessible eligibility information and an easy admission enquiry path.

Persona 02Parent / guardian

“I want to understand the college’s credibility and learning environment.”

Goals
Understand institutional recognition · Explore facilities · Learn about programmes · Access contact information.

UX needs
Trustworthy institutional content, clear academic information and visible contact details.

Persona 03Current student

“I want to stay informed about academic activities and teaching practice.”

Goals
Access academic information · Explore events · Discover faculty information · Find resources.

UX needs
Organised navigation, easy access to updates and consistent information structure.

06 / Key UX decisions

Turning academic information into a clear experience.

The key page areas give visitors context first, then help them explore programmes and decide what to do next.

01 / IntroductionContext

Establish the institution’s purpose

Present the college background, location and educational purpose together, so visitors understand the institution before moving into programme details.

02 / Vision & missionValues

Give institutional values their own space

Use dedicated visual sections for the college’s stated focus on quality education, rural upliftment, student development and socially responsible educators.

03 / ProgrammesDiscovery

Separate B.Ed and M.Ed pathways

Use distinct programme cards and detail sections. The supplied content lists a two-year duration for each programme and separate qualifying-degree requirements.

04 / Why choose usBenefits

Make key information scannable

Present the supplied points — faculty, teaching practice, career guidance, smart classrooms, fee information and placement support — as concise content cards.

05 / CareersOutcomes

Connect education with possible pathways

Show the career directions listed in the college material: school teacher, lecturer or professor, educational counsellor, academic coordinator, and higher studies or research.

06 / AdmissionsAction

Place enquiry actions after useful context

Use the “Train to Teach, Inspire to Lead” message with Apply Online and telephone enquiry actions after visitors have explored the institution and programmes.

07 / ContactUtility

Make contact details easy to locate

Organise address, phone, email and supporting links into clear footer groups.

07 / User flow

Helping aspiring educators find the right programme.

A straightforward journey from first visit to an informed enquiry.

Visit homepage→Explore academics→Select B.Ed or M.Ed→Review eligibility & duration→Explore facilities & careers→Apply / Contact

13 / Learnings & conclusion

Designing for the people who shape future generations.

A teacher education website should communicate not only what students can study, but also the educators they can become.

This project explored how digital design can communicate an institution’s purpose and educational values. Teacher education connects academic learning with practical teaching experience and professional responsibility, so the website needs to make those relationships visible.

Working across 26 desktop and mobile screens strengthened the focus on information architecture, academic content organisation, institutional storytelling, programme discovery, responsive UI design and reusable components.
