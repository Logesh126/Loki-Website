Building a practical, career-focused digital experience for polytechnic education.

01 / Overview

A digital experience centred on practical education.

Mother Terasa Polytechnic College focuses on technical education, practical training and developing skilled professionals for modern industries.

The redesign creates a modern, accessible and career-oriented website experience. It gives prospective students and parents a clearer way to explore diploma programmes, understand practical learning opportunities, discover facilities and access admission information.

The project covers 34 screens across desktop and mobile, with a consistent visual language throughout the website.

02 / The challenge

Make technical education easier to understand and explore.

A polytechnic website needs to communicate its practical-learning focus while helping visitors navigate course options, facilities, career paths and admission requirements.

05 / User understanding

Three proposed visitor profiles.

These personas are design tools based on the information needs represented in the website. They are not based on documented user interviews.

Persona 01 / School leaver

Exploring diploma courses

Wants to compare engineering disciplines, understand eligibility and practical learning, explore career directions and contact admissions.

Persona 02 / Parent or guardian

Evaluating the institution

Looks for the college background, leadership, laboratories, transport, hostel, student support and reliable contact details.

Persona 03 / Technical learner

Looking for hands-on learning

Wants to understand workshops, equipment, project-based learning, industrial visits, technical events and possible career paths.

06 / Key page decisions

Designing each page around a clear visitor need.

The supplied designs organise the content into focused page experiences rather than placing every institutional detail on a single long page.

Homepage

Build the Future with Engineering

The homepage introduces the college through a hero, institutional overview, engineering disciplines, infrastructure, career opportunities, campus life, gallery, testimonials and an admission call to action. The supplied design displays 25+ years of excellence, 5,000+ successful alumni, 5 engineering departments and 100% practical exposure. These are website claims and require institutional verification before publication.

About Us

Communicate the institution's identity

Institutional overview, mission, vision, core values, quality policy and leadership messages are separated into clear sections. The supplied design names Founder Shri. R. Chinnathambi, Chairman Shri. R. C. Uthayakumar and Principal Prof. P. Murugesan.

Academics

Explain how students learn

Teaching methodology, laboratory learning, project-based learning and industry exposure are grouped separately from continuous internal assessment, practical examinations, board examinations, project evaluation, mentorship, remedial classes and career guidance.

Engineering disciplines

Help students explore a direction

Five discipline cards introduce Computer Engineering, Electronics & Communication, Electrical & Electronics, Mechanical Engineering and Civil Engineering. Each uses a concise subject introduction, career scope and an Explore Programme action.

Facilities

Make practical learning visible

Nine facility cards cover Computer Lab, Electronics Lab, Electrical Machines Lab, Mechanical Workshop, Civil Engineering Lab, CAD Design Lab, Library, Transportation and Hostel, with a cafeteria also included.

Campus Life

Show experiences beyond the classroom

Technical symposiums, project exhibitions, industrial visits, skill-development workshops, sports and cultural events, NSS camps, social service and technical clubs such as robotics and coding are presented as part of student life.

Contact Us

Make admission enquiries accessible

A two-column desktop layout places contact information and the enquiry form alongside a map. The form includes name, phone number, email address and message fields with a Submit Enquiry action.

15 / Content checks

Details to confirm before public release.

The following inconsistencies were identified in the supplied content and designs. They are flagged for verification rather than silently corrected.

ItemObservation / action
College nameThe designs use both “Mother Terasa” and “Mother Teresa”. Confirm official spelling and apply it consistently.
Establishment / experienceAbout Us mentions over 25 years, while the Principal's message says the college has been committed to education since 2006. Verify the establishment year.
Contact mapThe map label identifies “Mother Terasa College of Education” on the Polytechnic contact page. Confirm and correct the map label.
Navigation labelOne homepage section uses “Life@MPTC”, while navigation uses “Life@MTPC”. Confirm the intended abbreviation.
StatisticsVerify 25+ years, 5,000+ alumni and 100% practical exposure before publication.
AccreditationsConfirm the documents and current recognition details before presenting them as official claims.
