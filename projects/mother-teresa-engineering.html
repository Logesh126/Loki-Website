01 / Project overview

A clearer route through engineering education.

The redesign organises academic programmes, departments, institutional information, approvals and admissions into a consistent experience across desktop and mobile.

Mother Terasa College of Engineering & Technology (MTCET) focuses on technical education, innovation, research and preparing students for professional careers. The project involved redesigning the institution's website to make important information easier to discover and understand.

The work covers 144 screens — 72 desktop and 72 mobile — with a consistent visual language and responsive content structure.

02 / The challenge

More than a list of courses.

Visitors need to understand the institution, compare programmes, explore departments, review facilities, find approval information and contact admissions without getting lost.

01 / Discovery

Programme discovery

Present engineering programmes in a clear, accessible format and help visitors move from course overview to programme details.

02 / Navigation

Department navigation

Organise multiple engineering disciplines into a predictable structure without overwhelming visitors.

03 / Trust

Institutional credibility

Give appropriate visibility to leadership messages, approvals, affiliations and institutional information.

04 / Access

Documents and admissions

Make approval documents easier to locate and keep admission and contact actions accessible.

Design assumptions shaped by visitor needs.

These are proposed design personas based on likely information needs represented in the website. They are not based on documented user interviews.

Persona 01

Prospective engineering student

Wants to compare programmes, understand duration and medium of instruction, explore departments and laboratories, and find a straightforward way to enquire or apply.

Persona 02

Wants to understand institutional credibility, approvals, affiliations, leadership, facilities, campus environment and contact information.

Persona 03

Current student

Needs quick access to academic information, department details, events and campus resources through a website that works well on mobile.

06 / Key pages & UX decisions

Key journeys across the website.

The interface balances institutional storytelling with practical routes to programmes, documents and enquiries.

Homepage — a strong first impression

The homepage brings together the institutional overview, vision and mission, leadership messages, recognition and affiliation information, and admissions actions.

  • Present the institution name and primary actions prominently.
  • Give visitors direct routes to explore programmes and apply.
  • Separate vision and mission into readable content areas.
  • Use leadership messages to communicate institutional direction.
  • Give recognition and affiliation information a dedicated section.

Academics — programme discovery

The B.E. programme page presents six undergraduate programmes in a structured card layout: Civil Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; Computer Science & Engineering; Electronics & Communication Engineering; Electrical & Electronics Engineering; and Robotics and Automation. Each card includes the degree name, four-year duration, English medium and a View Program action, with syllabus download links.

  • Use consistent programme cards to support scanning.
  • Keep course duration and medium visible.
  • Provide a clear next step and accessible syllabus documents.

Departments — organising disciplines

The departments page lists nine academic areas: Civil Engineering; Computer Science & Engineering; Artificial Intelligence & Data Science; Computer Science & Business System; Electronics & Communication Engineering; Electrical & Electronics Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; Robotics and Automation; and Science & Humanities.

  • Group departments in a consistent card grid.
  • Use short descriptions and a View Program action.
  • Reinforce the academic context with department-related imagery.

About Us — institutional trust

The page combines the institutional overview, vision, mission, leadership messages and recognition information. The supplied content states that the institution was established in 2009 and identifies AICTE approval, Anna University affiliation and ISO 9001:2015 certification; current claims require confirmation against institutional records.

Accreditations & Committees — document access

The supplied design includes AICTE student feedback and Extension of Approval documents from academic years 2025–26 through 2009–10. A repeatable document-card pattern makes the year and download action easy to identify.

Contact Us — simplifying enquiries

The contact experience includes the institutional address, telephone numbers, email addresses, map and location information, and an enquiry form with name, email, phone number, department and message. The two-column layout separates contact information from the form and provides Call Us and Send an Enquiry actions.

12 / Key learnings

What this project reinforced.

Balance information and storytellingInstitutional websites need to serve both academic discovery and institutional context.
Make programme discovery straightforwardStudents benefit from clear programme and department pathways.
Give trust information visibilityApprovals and affiliations should be easy to find and understand.
Structure document-heavy pagesConsistent cards and clear download actions improve document discovery.
Design responsive hierarchyMobile design should preserve information hierarchy, not merely shrink desktop layouts.
Build reusable componentsShared patterns support consistency across a large multi-page website.
