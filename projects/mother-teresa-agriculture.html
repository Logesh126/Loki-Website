01 / Overview

Bringing agricultural education closer to the digital world.

A college website should communicate more than academic information. It should help visitors understand how scientific knowledge connects with practical experience.

Agricultural education brings together crop cultivation, soil science, modern farming techniques, agricultural technology and sustainable practices. The digital experience needs to make these different parts of the institution easier to understand and explore.

The objective of this redesign was to create a modern institutional experience that communicates the college’s agricultural identity while making programmes, facilities and practical learning opportunities easier to discover.

02 / The challenge

Communicating agricultural education beyond the classroom.

The challenge was to organise academic, practical and institutional information into a clear experience for prospective students, parents and current students.

01 / DiscoveryAcademics

Academic programme discovery

Make agricultural courses, departments and programme information easier to locate and compare.

02 / ExperienceField learning

Practical learning visibility

Give field activities, crop cultivation and hands-on learning a stronger place in the content hierarchy.

03 / FacilitiesInfrastructure

Agricultural facilities

Make laboratories, research spaces and agricultural infrastructure easier to discover.

Communicating modern agriculture

Present agriculture as a discipline connected to science, technology, innovation and sustainability.

How might we create an engaging digital experience that helps students discover agricultural programmes, understand practical field learning and explore modern agricultural education?

04 / Research & discovery

Understanding the agricultural education audience.

The experience was framed around three audience groups and the information they may need from an institutional website.

Persona 01 / Prospective student

Aspiring agriculture student

“I want to understand agricultural courses and what practical skills I can develop during my studies.”
Goals
  • Explore agricultural programmes
  • Understand academic departments
  • Discover fieldwork opportunities
  • View facilities and laboratories
Needs to address

Clear course information and stronger visibility of practical learning.

Persona 02 / Parent

Parent or guardian

“I want to understand the college’s learning environment, facilities and future opportunities.”
Goals
  • Evaluate the institution
  • Explore campus facilities
  • Understand academic opportunities
  • Find admission information
Needs to address

Well-organised institutional information and accessible facility details.

Persona 03 / Existing student

Current student

“I want to explore academic information, field activities and agricultural events.”
Goals
  • Access department information
  • Discover field activities
  • Explore research and events
  • Find campus updates
Needs to address

Consistent page structures and easier access to updates.

These are illustrative design personas based on likely website needs, not documented interview participants. The observations below are design-analysis hypotheses, not measured research findings.

09 / Responsive design

Creating a consistent experience across devices.

The responsive approach aims to preserve the readability of academic content and the visual impact of agricultural imagery.

NavigationFull desktop navigation adapts to a compact mobile menu.
Programme cardsMulti-column desktop cards become stacked mobile cards.
Hero imageryWide desktop compositions adapt to vertical mobile layouts.
Image galleriesLarge desktop galleries become responsive mobile layouts.
Information sectionsHorizontal desktop sections flow vertically on smaller screens.
FooterMulti-column footer content becomes a stacked layout.

11 / Learnings

Designing for an education system rooted in the real world.

Agricultural education extends beyond classrooms, bringing together academic information, practical field experiences, scientific learning and sustainability.

This project helped me explore how a digital experience can communicate a specialised educational discipline through a consistent visual language and a structured information architecture.

The key learning was understanding how visual storytelling and UX structure can make specialised educational information more approachable without losing its depth.
