Mother Teresa Group / UI/UX Design / 07

College of Physical Education.

A dedicated website experience for college of physical education, designed as part of the wider Mother Teresa Group digital ecosystem.

College of Physical Education
UI/UX Designer
12 desktop + 12 mobile screens
Figma
01 / Overview

A website shaped around its institution.

A sports and physical education institution needs a clear way to communicate programmes, facilities and campus information.

This website forms one part of a wider digital ecosystem for Mother Teresa Group. Each institution has its own audience, information needs and identity, so the interface is treated as an individual experience rather than a repeated page template.

Balance an energetic institutional identity with a readable structure for academic and practical information.

02 / Design preview

The desktop experience.

A preview of the original desktop homepage design. The supplied design collection for this institution contains 12 desktop + 12 mobile screens.

The design work focuses on presenting institutional information through a clear visual hierarchy, readable content sections and navigation that helps visitors move through the website.

03 / Design focus

What the interface needs to communicate.

The content structure and visual treatment are guided by the institution’s role within the group.

Institutional identityEstablish the institution and its place within the wider group.
Academic informationMake programmes and education-related information easier to explore.
Visitor orientationHelp students, parents and visitors find key information.
Admissions and contactKeep important next-step information accessible.
