01 / Project overview

Where academic learning meets athletic development.

A physical education institution needs to communicate both academic learning and practical sports training. The website brings programme information, sports disciplines, eligibility and career directions into a structured experience.

Mother Terasa College of Physical Education (MTCPE) focuses on physical education, sports science, fitness development, coaching and sports management. The redesign aims to help prospective students understand the B.P.E.S. programme, curriculum, sports disciplines and possible professional pathways.

Unlike a conventional college website, the experience gives practical learning and athletic activities a prominent role in the institution's digital storytelling. The design scope includes 12 desktop and 12 mobile screens.

02 / The challenge

Communicating the full value of sports education.

Visitors need to understand what they will study, which sports they will experience, how the programme is structured and what directions may be available after graduation.

06 / Key pages & UX decisions

A sports-led journey from discovery to enquiry.

The homepage connects programme information, academic structure, sports exposure and career exploration with admission actions.

Homepage — making sports education the centre

The homepage introduces the institution through a sports-focused hero featuring outdoor athletic activity and the B.P.E.S. programme. The supplied hero presents Bachelor of Physical Education & Sports (B.P.E.S.), 3 Years, Undergraduate and Full-Time, with Apply Now and Download Brochures actions.

  • Use sports photography to communicate the institution's specialisation.
  • Present programme duration and study mode near the main heading.
  • Keep Apply Now and Download Brochures prominent.
  • Organise long-form information into distinct sections.

Programme overview — explaining B.P.E.S.

The overview introduces physical education, sports sciences, fitness development, coaching and sports management. The supplied design displays 8+ sports disciplines, 6 semesters and 100% practical training. These are design claims and require verification before publication.

  • Pair a concise programme overview with sports-related imagery.
  • Use statistics to make programme information scannable.
  • Keep the focus on what prospective students may learn.

Why choose this programme — communicating learning benefits

Six benefit cards present Sports Science Foundation, Practical Training, Coaching Expertise, Leadership Development, Fitness Education and Career Readiness. A consistent six-card layout, short headings and concise supporting descriptions help visitors compare the benefits.

What you will learn — curriculum overview

The supplied design introduces eight learning areas: Sports Science; Human Anatomy & Physiology; Sports Psychology; Sports Sociology; Health, Fitness & Nutrition; Sports Management; Coaching Techniques; and Performance Analysis. The two-column subject layout separates these topics from the semester-wise structure.

Curriculum structure — the three-year journey

The programme is presented as a three-year, six-semester journey, with year selectors for Years 1, 2 and 3. The visible first-year curriculum includes:

Semester 1: Foundations of Physical Education and Sports; Anatomy and Physiology; Theories of Track and Field – I; Marching, Calisthenics, Minor Games and Drills.

Semester 2: Theories of Major Games – I; Sports Psychology and Sociology; Yoga Education.

The supplied visible content provides detailed subjects for the first year only. Remaining semester details should come from the official curriculum, not inference.

Sports disciplines — practical training in focus

The image-led gallery shows outdoor training and student participation. Visible labels include Athletics, Football, Volleyball, Cricket, Throwball, Shuttle and Handball. Consistent image cards with clear labels make practical activity a prominent part of the institution's identity.

Eligibility — making entry requirements clear

The supplied design displays a pass in +2 (HSC) from a recognised board, a maximum age of 21 years and three years' age relaxation for SC/ST students. Each criterion is separated into a card for quick scanning. Verify these requirements against current admission regulations.

Career opportunities — showing possible directions

The supplied design lists Assistant Professor, Sports Scientist, Research Scholar, Performance Analyst, Sports Administrator, Sports Consultant, Strength & Conditioning Specialist and Sports Development Officer. These are presented as potential career directions, not guaranteed employment or placements.

Admissions CTA — encouraging the next step

The closing section uses the message “Start Your Journey in Sports Education” and displays Admissions Open 2026–27, with Apply Now and Contact for Admissions actions. Update the intake year to the relevant admission cycle before publishing.

Contact Us — making communication accessible

The supplied contact design uses a two-column desktop layout, with institutional details and a map on the left and an enquiry form on the right. It displays the address Mettusalai, Illupur, Tamil Nadu – 622102; email mtcpe2015@gmail.com; phone +91 94433 72151; and additional numbers +91 99429 88544 and +91 99429 04531. The form includes name, email, phone number and message fields. Confirm all details before public use.

08 / Visual design direction

Red accents. Soft pink surfaces. Real sports imagery.

The supplied designs use red as the primary accent, supported by white and pale-pink backgrounds. Exact colour values should be confirmed against the original Figma styles.

Visual characteristics include red accents for buttons, headings and key highlights; soft pink section backgrounds; white academic and curriculum cards; rounded containers with subtle borders and shadows; sports photography as the main storytelling element; clear typography and consistent spacing; and a strong red footer.
