The challenge

Marsforce NextGen operates at the intersection of lpg distribution, operational support and modern technology. Its proposition brings together distributor operations, crm, sales automation, ai-enabled insights, field-force management, customer engagement and commercial lpg solutions.

The challenge was to communicate this broad business model without making the website feel like a disconnected list of services. The experience needed to establish Marsforce as a technology partner that understands the lpg distribution ecosystem and supports businesses as they evolve.

How might we translate a complex mix of energy operations, business support and technology into a clear, credible and easy-to-navigate digital experience?

Understanding the business

Before shaping the interface, I focused on understanding the relationship between the company's industry context and its technology offering. The business proposition spans several connected capabilities:

  • lpg distribution and operational support.
  • Technology-enabled business management and crm.
  • Customer engagement and lifecycle management.
  • Sales automation, analytics and business development.
  • Field-force coordination and operational visibility.
  • Digital marketing and commercial lpg solutions.

This helped frame the company around one connected proposition rather than presenting each capability as an unrelated service.

Positioning direction: Marsforce as the technology partner behind smarter lpg distribution.

Research & business analysis

The research and analysis were centred on the lpg distribution context, the activities a distributor manages and the audiences the corporate website needed to address. These considerations informed the content strategy; they are not presented as independently measured research findings.

Industry and operational context

We considered distributor and branch operations, domestic and commercial customer management, refill and delivery coordination, sales tracking, field activities, customer communication, complaint handling and operational compliance.

Audience considerations

Primary: lpg distributors, distribution business owners and commercial lpg operators.

Secondary: potential business partners, commercial customers and organisations exploring technology-enabled operations.

Defining the strategy

I used three guiding objectives to shape the content and interface direction.

01 — Build trust

Create a professional, established corporate presence that speaks to business owners and decision-makers.

02 — Simplify complexity

Organise a wide range of business services and technology capabilities into clear, understandable sections.

03 — Communicate business value

Explain how technology supports distributor operations and business growth, rather than relying on feature lists or technical jargon.

Design direction: a clean, modern corporate experience with a clear content hierarchy, confident typography and consistent use of the existing green-led Marsforce identity.

Information architecture

The homepage was planned as a continuous, single-scroll narrative. Visitors are introduced to the business first, then guided through its services, platform capabilities, values and contact options.

Hero & positioning→Business role→Services→Platform capabilities→Technology & ai→Vision→Contact

The wider site also needed dedicated destinations for information that deserved more depth than the homepage could provide.

Leadership

A separate leadership page gives the founder and leadership team a dedicated space, avoiding repetitive About Us content on the homepage.

Careers

A recruitment destination supports job categories, individual job descriptions, application actions and a useful no-openings state.

Designing the homepage

01 — Hero: establish the proposition

“Powering India's lpg Distributors with Smart Technology” leads with the industry Marsforce serves and the role technology plays in its offering. Supporting copy connects automation and operational support to more efficient distributor businesses.

02 — The technology partner behind the scenes

This section introduces Marsforce as a partner supporting the operations behind lpg distribution before visitors encounter individual services.

03 — What we do for your business

The proposition is grouped around two understandable themes: Backend Automation and Sales & Support. This makes the offering easier to scan without flattening it into a long service list.

04 — Smart lpg distribution capabilities

Structured feature cards communicate the connected operational capabilities and show how the ecosystem supports multiple stages of distributor operations.

05 — Control, visibility & compliance

This section frames operational oversight and informed decision-making as important outcomes of connected systems.

06 — One connected platform

A darker green feature panel creates a visual pause and reinforces the idea of bringing business activities together in one ecosystem.

07 — The technology backbone

A visual explanation helps visitors understand how the platform connects business functions without overloading them with technical architecture.

08 — Sales growth powered by ai

ai is positioned as a practical business-enabling capability, connected to sales insights, recommendations, automation and performance.

09 — Commercial segment

A dedicated section communicates the relevance of Marsforce's proposition to commercial lpg requirements as well as domestic distribution.

10 — What drives us

Vision, mission and promise content introduces the thinking behind the business. Floating card elements provide a change of rhythm within the structured corporate layout.

11 — Contact and faq

A clear business enquiry section, contact details, form fields and FAQs give distributors and partners a direct next step and address common questions.

Visual direction

The visual language balances the reliability expected from an energy-sector business with the forward-looking character of a technology company. The existing green-led Marsforce identity remains the anchor.

  • Green brand accents for emphasis and key actions.
  • White and light neutral surfaces for clarity and breathing space.
  • Dark sections used selectively to introduce contrast.
  • Subtle grey cards, rounded containers and simple iconography.
  • Strong headline hierarchy, readable body copy and consistent spacing.

The goal was to make a content-rich corporate website feel structured, approachable and professional.

Leadership experience

The leadership page adds a human dimension to the corporate identity. Rather than repeating the homepage's business overview, it focuses on the people and vision behind Marsforce.

Page structure

  • Leadership introduction: “The Visionaries Behind Marsforce”.
  • Founder's message and profile for S. Karthikeyan, B.E., Founder and Managing Director.
  • Leadership profiles covering Founder / md & ceo, coo, cto, cfo, cmo and hr Manager roles.
  • A closing section around the principles “Technology Simplifies”, “Data Drives Decisions” and “Customer Success”.

The founder profile content supplied for the project described 14+ years of entrepreneurial and business leadership experience, with exposure to lpg distribution, energy management, petroleum retail, ev charging infrastructure, commercial lpg, ai-powered crm, sales automation and business development.

The role structure also keeps the Founder, Managing Director and ceo titles with the same individual, rather than implying separate people.

Careers experience

The careers area was planned as a recruitment destination rather than a simple contact form.

Job categories

Business & Operations, Sales & Revenue, Marketing, Technology & Software, and Support & Administration.

Individual job description pages

Role overview→Responsibilities→Requirements→Bonus skills→Why Marsforce→Apply

Each role page follows a consistent structure while allowing the responsibilities and requirements to be tailored to the position. A general 1–3 years' experience range was a content guideline where appropriate, not a universal requirement for every role.

No-openings state

When there are no active vacancies, the page can communicate “No Openings Right Now” while still inviting candidates to share their cv through the hr contact channel. This avoids leaving the recruitment experience as an empty page.

Pre-launch exploration

During the early development phase, a temporary Coming Soon page was explored to give the domain a professional presence while the main website was being developed.

The visual exploration began with a space-inspired direction and was subsequently adapted towards a light, white-themed aesthetic to align with the corporate website direction and stakeholder preference.

Outcome & reflection

The project brought the company's business proposition, service ecosystem and supporting corporate information into one connected website experience. The design direction covered the main homepage narrative alongside dedicated Leadership and Careers destinations.

It brought together business positioning, information architecture, content hierarchy, visual design and website implementation as parts of one digital experience.

The biggest learning: a corporate website is not just a collection of sections. It is a translation of how a business works, what it values and how it wants to be understood.

Marsforce challenged me to connect the worlds of energy, operations, technology, automation and business growth — and communicate them through a coherent digital presence.
