01 / Overview

A school experience designed for parents and students.

Mother Terasa Matriculation Higher Secondary School is an educational institution in Mettusalai, Illupur, Tamil Nadu.

Unlike a college website, a school website needs to communicate with both students and parents. Parents want to understand the school's educational approach, facilities, academic support and admission process, while students need an engaging view of learning and campus life.

The redesign brings these information areas together in a structured digital experience across 22 screens — 11 desktop and 11 mobile.

05 / User understanding

Three proposed visitor profiles.

These personas are design tools based on the information needs reflected in the website. They are not based on documented user interviews.

Persona 01 / Parent or guardian

Evaluating a school

Wants to understand the educational philosophy, academic stages, facilities, teaching methods, grade eligibility, admissions and location.

Persona 02 / Prospective student

Exploring school life

Looks for the learning environment, sports, activities, campus facilities and events; wants to understand what learning looks like at each stage.

Persona 03 / Existing parent

Looking for useful updates

Needs quick access to school information and contact details, and wants to see student activities, achievements and campus updates.

06 / Key page decisions

Page experiences shaped around the questions visitors bring.

The supplied designs use a clear sequence: introduce the school, explain the learning journey, show the campus experience and make admissions accessible.

Homepage / First impression

Nurturing Young Minds for a Brighter Tomorrow.

The homepage introduces the school with a hero, Explore Curriculum and Admissions Open actions, welcome section, Why Choose Us highlights, five academic stages, achievements, learning approach, campus facilities, gallery, parent testimonials, FAQs and admission/contact actions. Alternating content sections, cards and image-led areas help make a long homepage easier to explore.

About Us / Institutional story

Build trust through context and leadership.

The page presents vision, mission, history and leadership. The supplied vision describes becoming a nationally recognised institution for quality education that nurtures academic excellence, character, leadership and lifelong learning. The mission focuses on holistic education, innovative teaching, modern infrastructure and a supportive environment.

Institutional timeline

Make the school's journey scannable.

A timeline presents milestones: 1998 — school established; 2005 — Higher Secondary classes introduced; 2012 — new science block and sports complex inaugurated; 2015 — student achievement recognition; 2024 — smart classrooms and digital learning initiatives. Verify all milestones before publication.

Leadership

Give leadership messages their own space.

The page concludes with three leadership messages from the Founder, Chairman and Principal. Dedicated sections combine leadership portraits with supporting text rather than placing all institutional context in one long paragraph.

Academics / Learning journey

Present five stages of education.

The Academics page explains the learning structure from Kindergarten to Higher Secondary, with grade ranges and learning priorities visible for each stage.

Teaching methodology

Explain how students learn.

Five approaches are presented as individual cards: Interactive Learning, Project-Based Learning, Smart Classrooms, Activity-Based Education and Collaborative Learning. Each pairs a recognisable label with a concise explanation.

Academic support

Make student support discoverable.

Mentoring, Remedial Classes, Career Guidance and Counselling each receive a dedicated card. Subtle accent variations distinguish services while retaining a consistent layout.

Why Choose Us

Summarise the school's strengths.

Six highlights — Academic Excellence, Experienced Faculty, Holistic Development, Safe Environment, Modern Infrastructure and Individual Attention — provide a quick overview for parents comparing schools.

Campus Life and Gallery

Bring the school environment to life.

Library, Labs, Sports, Auditorium and Cafeteria are presented as five campus facilities. A separate gallery grid gives visitors a visual overview of student activities and the school community.

Parent testimonials

Add a parent perspective.

Three testimonial cards focus on teaching quality, infrastructure, dedicated teachers, management and children's confidence. Parent names and children's grades are supporting context in the supplied design; confirm consent and wording before public use.

Admissions and Contact

Make enquiries straightforward.

The two-column desktop layout places school contact information and a location map on the left, with the admission enquiry form on the right. The form includes Parent's Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Child's Name, Applying for Grade and Any Questions?, followed by Submit Enquiry.

14 / Content checks

Details to verify before public release.

These are checks identified in the supplied material. They are flagged for confirmation rather than silently corrected.

ItemObservation / action
School nameThe PDFs use “Martic. Hr. Sec. School” in several places. Confirm whether this is a spelling error for “Matriculation Higher Secondary School”.
Establishment yearThe About Us page states 1998. Verify this against official records.
TimelineConfirm the milestones for 2005, 2012, 2015 and 2024.
StatisticsThe homepage displays 3,200+ students, 60+ faculty members, 98% academic success and 25+ years of excellence. Verify these claims.
TestimonialsConfirm parent names, consent and testimonial wording before publishing.
Contact detailsConfirm phone number, email and map location are current.
Footer taglineThe supplied tagline references practical learning and innovation, which may be better suited to a technical institution. Consider a school-focused tagline if the client approves.
