01 / Overview

A website shaped around its institution.

A broad academic offering needs clear pathways to departments, programmes, admissions and campus information.

This website forms one part of a wider digital ecosystem for Mother Teresa Group. Each institution has its own audience, information needs and identity, so the interface is treated as an individual experience rather than a repeated page template.

Help prospective students and parents understand the college, explore academic options and find essential information without losing the institution’s identity.

03 / The challenge

Make a large academic offering easier to navigate.

The website needs to serve prospective students, parents, current students and visitors, each arriving with different information needs.

01 / Discovery

Programme exploration

Help visitors scan undergraduate options and move towards relevant programme details.

02 / Structure

Department navigation

Give multiple academic areas a consistent and understandable content structure.

03 / Confidence

Institutional credibility

Make research, accreditations, achievements and campus information easy to find.

04 / Engagement

Student experience

Bring campus life, activities and student testimonials into the wider story.

05 / Action

Admission pathways

Keep admission and contact actions visible at relevant points in the journey.

06 / Continuity

Responsive behaviour

Maintain hierarchy and access to key information across desktop and mobile.

Core design question: How might the website help students and parents discover programmes, understand the institution and find their next step without feeling overwhelmed?
04 / Audience & discovery

Designing around real visitor needs.

These are proposed audience profiles derived from the website’s purpose, not claims of completed interviews or user testing.

Persona 01Prospective student

“I want to find a programme that fits my future.”

Needs clear UG/PG discovery, programme details, admission information and an easy route to enquire.

Persona 02Parent / guardian

“I want to understand the college before making a decision.”

Needs institutional information, facilities, achievements and accessible contact details.

Persona 03Current student

“I want to stay connected with college information.”

Needs structured department content, research updates, activities and campus information.

Design opportunityInformation architecture

Turn content volume into clear pathways.

Use consistent navigation, predictable page patterns and focused calls to action.

08 / Responsive design

One experience across two screen sizes.

The project includes 73 desktop and 73 mobile screens. The mobile layouts adapt content priority and page structure for smaller viewports.

NavigationFull desktop navigation adapts to compact mobile navigation.
Programme discoveryMulti-column grids adapt to stacked cards.
Hero and contentWide compositions adapt to narrower responsive layouts.
FooterMulti-column information adapts to stacked sections.

10 / Reflection

What this project reinforced.

A large education website is not simply a collection of pages. It needs a repeatable structure that helps different audiences find the information relevant to them.

This project brought together information architecture, content hierarchy, responsive layout planning and reusable interface patterns across 146 screens. The central learning was to balance consistency across the website with the different needs of each content area.
